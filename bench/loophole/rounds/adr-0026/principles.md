These are the estate's own normative claims about holes, selections and refusals. Every line is
quoted or paraphrased from `NORTH-STAR.md` §3, from `CONTEXT.md` (the "Exemption", "Baseline",
"Hole", "Delta", "Control claim" and "Instrument fault" entries), from ADR-0020, from ADR-0026
point 5(b) (line 11), and from the ticket 07 principles for ADR-0022 (line 12). Nothing here is
invented for this run.

1. Everything is policy. There are no exemptions, no exemption ledger, no carve-outs for a named
   workload, at any scope, under any name. An allowance is either a conditional rule anyone can
   meet, or a cage with a price.

2. Everything is always caged. The cage spec is the only variable, and the price selects the
   spec. There is no gate. Something may be unable to run only because it does not fit its cage,
   never because it is deliberately denied.

3. One currency, proportionate to the organisation. Every impact and every response is priced
   in one currency, so a hardening control, a cage tier and an insurance transfer are comparable.
   Regulator penalties enter as published schemas applied to the adopter's own size.

4. A missing behaviour is priced. A control left unimplemented, a stale size fact or a revoked
   pin moves the price. Nothing refuses on a behaviour.

5. A missing instrument refuses. When nothing pinned can read or price the thing named (no price
   for a declared regime, no exchange rate for the date), the composition must not emit a number.

6. Selection is the risk-bearing act. An adopter selects a named baseline in the party artefact
   it signs, may add controls to it, and may remove one. The change is signed, party-wide and
   printed.

7. A control the adopter cannot meet is caged and priced, not dropped.

8. A claim belongs to whoever ships the implementation. A party may never claim against a policy
   another party ships.

9. A hole is a selected control no claim covers. It is priced, never refused and never counted.
   Implementing a control reduces the price. A hole no pinned weight names carries a named
   absence, never a zero.

10. A delta is a report of a priced move, never a wall. Each carries the adopter's own
    perspective and currency, and an amount or a named absence.

11. Nothing is hidden from the price. A change that hides a hole from the pound is an exemption
    by another name.

12. Disagreement with a rule is resolved by a pull request to the policy, in the open, under
    review. It is never resolved by a favour or a silent edit.
