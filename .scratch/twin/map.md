# Organisational digital twin + anticipatory governance — the whole thing

`wayfinder:map`

## Destination

A rigorous, comprehensive **digital twin of an organisation** and an **anticipatory
strategic-intelligence engine**: it senses *any* signal — external (quantum, memory cost, AI-model
access, sanctions, East–West realignment, climate, supply-chain, M&A) and internal (people, morale,
knowledge concentration, comp, working patterns) — maps it onto the org's Wardley/dependency
landscape, **war-games the what-ifs (fast-forward · rewind · play)**, prices every impact *and every
candidate response* in **one £ risk currency**, and recommends the **cheapest proportionate response
wherever it lives** — an HR lever, a security control, a strategic play. Versioned, attestable
governance is *one enactment arm*, not the point.

**Plan the whole thing to full ambition — everything is modelled. The demo scope is worked backwards
from the plan, later.** Built **skills-first, from basics**, on **real risk modelling and real
Wardley** — nothing inherited from the toy prior.

Done when: the whole is charted comprehensively (all workstreams, seams, and explicit acceptance
criteria), grounded in real methodology, with a worked-backwards demo slice — such that a build could
start any track without re-deciding its basis.

## Notes

Git holds all history; the **specs (`.scratch/talk-spec/spec.md`, `the-whole-model.md`) are the

**Settled framing (from charting grilling 2026-08-04 — do not re-litigate without the human):**
- **Destination = the rigorous real system**, not a talk. Optimising for a demo is exactly what
  produced the monorepo + toy risk; making the real, right, comprehensive thing the north star stops
  the shortcuts. The talk is a *byproduct*.
- **The reframe:** the project is an **org digital twin + anticipation engine**; versioned governance
  is one enactment arm (the IT/security slice).
- **Scope is total.** Any factor that can move the org's landscape is in scope — external and
  internal. Quantum was one example; nothing is excluded by category.
- **One £ risk currency → cross-domain comparison** is the payoff: a *pay rise* (HR control) and
  *security hardening* (tech control) become comparable options against the same modelled risk;
  pick the cheaper proportionate one. Governance stops being siloed.
- **Fear *and* opportunity.** The same intelligence that says "defend" surfaces the strategic play to
  *seize* (Wardley gameplay). The engine feeds strategy/roadmap, not just the risk register.
- **Falsifiability.** The twin's what-if projections are checked against **what actually
  materialises**; it earns trust by being wrong in public sometimes and calibrating.
- **Reflexive governance + ethics is a design requirement, not a footnote.** The twin governs its own
  surveillance and its own AI — DPIA, minimisation, transparency, consent, AI assurance. Sensing
  morale/comp/behaviour to predict insider risk is legally and ethically loaded and must be governed.
- **Synthetic substrate.** The org's operational + behavioural data (email, chat, commits, HR events,
  supply-chain, telemetry) is **AI-synthesised with realistic noise** — never real surveillance. This
  resolves the cardboard-org problem, sidesteps the real-data ethics for the *demo* (the ethics
  guardrail then governs *real* deployment), and — because *we* generate it — lets us **plant weak
  signals with known ground truth**, making signal-detection and the what-if→materialises loop
  *validatable* rather than asserted.
- **Multi-org reality.** Keep + use the **six real GitHub orgs** (`policy-as-versioned-*`). The
  enactment arm is real separate repos with real signed dependency pins — never a monorepo. Bin the
  `estate/` monorepo + the KinD clusters.
- **Skills-first, from basics; no rush; right + comprehensive; ambition = everything.**
- **Structure = disconnected parallel workstreams**, each with its **own checkpoints** and
  **explicit acceptance criteria**, wired at defined seams.
- **External falsifiability gates (added 2026-08-04, from the fable blind-spots pass).** Magnum-opus
  governs *scope* — no external gate on ambition or coverage. But a small, non-negotiable set of
  external *reality* gates governs the **honesty** claim: a **pre-registered forecast book** on
  real-world externals (scored later against what happens), **blind/adversarial planting** (the grader
  never saw the ground truth), **one external witness**, and a **frozen portfolio**. Own standard = how
  comprehensive; reality = whether it is true. This closes fable's meta-critique: otherwise every loop
  — validation, calibration, falsification, and the yardstick itself — closes through one mind.
- **History IS the external ground truth (added 2026-08-04).** Reconstruct the flagship's **real,
  documented history** as a time-series of Wardley/dependency states; back-test the anticipation engine
  by rewinding to a real past state, fast-forwarding, and scoring projected movement against what
  *actually* happened. The org's past is surprise we did not author — the strongest falsifiability
  mechanism. Implies the flagship needs a **real historical spine** (a single real org's public
  history); only the internal behavioural substrate is synthesised. "If we don't know where we've been,
  we can't know where we're going."

- **Backtest ground truth must be contemporaneous + adversarial (added 2026-08-04, from wave-1 OSINT + fable contrarian).** For the history backtest, prefer *adversarially-produced, timestamped* records — court/inquiry/examiner reports, short-seller theses, analyst dissent, market-priced short interest — over *cooperatively-produced survivor narratives* (insider books written with company access bake hindsight into the corpus). Selection must weight **temporal resolution** (is there timestamped public cadence to score *when* the twin should have flagged?) and **adversariality of record**, not just story depth. Corollary: a living, rich **flagship** (whole-org: fear AND opportunity, behavioural substrate) is paired with a **dedicated collapsed-firm backtest org** (market-priced / inquiry answer key) in the portfolio — two different jobs, neither org does both.
- **Two co-flagships (2026-08-04, resolves ticket 06): Netflix + Intel.** Netflix = the *retrospective/whole-engine* subject (rewind, fear-AND-seize on dated history, deep behavioural substrate, quarterly temporal resolution). Intel = the *live/forward* subject (a genuinely unresolved 2026 existential arc → falsifiable FORWARD forecasts, the pre-registered gate on a real org). The twin proves itself BOTH ways — backward (Netflix + the collapsed suite) and forward (Intel).
- **Shared world graph + per-org private overlays (2026-08-04).** One typed knowledge graph spined on the Wardley value-chain; a **shared world layer** (landscape, technologies, markets, geopolitics) plus **per-org overlays each org owns and never shares**. This is simultaneously the multi-tenant/data-sovereignty shape and the **credibility-theory structure** (industry prior = world layer, sparse own-data = overlay) — cross-org learning without private facts leaving the tenant.
- **Competing world models are first-class (2026-08-04).** Three senses: the org's **believed** map (can be wrong — believed-vs-actual delta *is* the anticipation failure, cf. Nokia), **rival forecasts** held simultaneously with credences (Knightian uncertainty on the evolution axis; the source of scenarios), and **revealed truth** (retrospective; the backtest key). Scoring pairs them: belief-vs-revealed = did the org see it; forecast-vs-revealed = was the twin right; belief-vs-forecast = what to tell the org now.
- **Co-registered forecasting is our contamination-proof external gate (2026-08-05, from `research/prediction-markets.md`).** The twin emits blind forecasts on the same questions/timestamps/resolutions as liquid prediction markets and both are scored on the same set. **Forward-dated questions cannot be in any training corpus**, so this escapes the memorisation problem the historical backtests carry — supplying volume, cadence and no-selection. **Honest scope limit: it tests general world-forecasting, NOT the org-twin causal machinery**, so the "loops close through one mind" critique narrows without closing. Consume market **price moves** as world-layer signals; never treat price **levels** as probabilities (favourite–longshot bias is rejected-unbiased in every subsample, worst in the deep tail we care about).
- **WARNING — accuracy does not save an instrument (2026-08-05).** Corporate prediction markets at Google/Ford **beat their own experts by up to 25%% MSE reduction and were killed anyway** — by manager incentives and information control, not by being wrong. This is aimed directly at the argue-with-it bet below: a better artefact does **not** automatically win the argument. Organisational adoption is a distinct risk the twin must model *about itself* (ticket 10) rather than assume away.
- **Code is disposable; the decisions endure (2026-08-05).** The durable artefacts are the versioned model and the decision record; code is nearer a *derived* artefact than an authored one, so replacing it is normal rather than wasteful. **Never married to previous investments.** Paired guard against the walking-skeleton failure modes: each resolved ticket's **full acceptance criteria stay the yardstick** (a skeleton satisfies a *slice*, never redefines them), and every capability carries a **depth grade** (stub/partial/full) against its owning ticket — so "done" always means done against the decision, never against the demo.
- **The twin is an artefact to ARGUE WITH, not an oracle (2026-08-05) — the unifying principle.** The point of a Wardley map is to give people something to debate that is *distanced from the human stories and emotion*: the disagreement is externalised onto the artefact rather than running between people. **A single number ends a conversation; a map sustains one.** This is *why* the design refuses everywhere to collapse plurality — competing world models, rival causal accounts, ensemble spread, a trade-off curve instead of a verdict, contestability as a first-class workflow. Terminating the argument would destroy the thing's actual function.
- **The twin is a WEATHER FORECAST (2026-08-04).** Forecasts are probabilistic; "not everything we predict will happen" is correct behaviour, not a defect. **Individual forecasts are neither right nor wrong — the forecaster is calibrated or not**, scored by proper scoring rules + reliability diagrams over *many* forecasts. Implication: the twin must make **continuous, dated, low-stakes forecasts at varied confidence**, since calibration can't be judged from a few dramatic calls. Rival world-models/causal accounts are the **ensemble** — their spread IS the uncertainty, not noise to resolve away; disagreement is legitimate and adjudicated by the calibration record over time.
- **Parametric contamination is a backtest pillar (2026-08-04).** An LLM twin "flagging" a famous collapse cannot be distinguished from memorisation. Prefer low-notoriety answer keys (Carillion, NMC Health); make Enron a *contamination control* (rubric on Enron vs an obscure key → a measured memorisation-leakage discount applied to all backtest scores); gate all information at time-T.
