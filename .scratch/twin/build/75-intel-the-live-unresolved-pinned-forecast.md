# 75 — Intel: the live, unresolved, pinned forecast

**What to build:** **The most honest thing in the demo.** A genuine *unresolved* forward forecast — emitted, pinned,
signed — where **we do not know the answer either**.

It cannot be scored yet, and **saying so on screen is the strongest demonstration of the
falsifiability claim**. A dated prediction someone can come back and check beats any
retrospective.

**Blocked by:** 70, 58

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Reading list:** Decision tickets 06, 22. Spec story 92.

- [ ] A forward forecast on Intel, pinned and signed before any resolution.
- [ ] Explicitly unscoreable, and the artefact says so rather than showing a placeholder.
- [ ] The resolution date and the checking procedure are published with it.
- [ ] It is emitted through the normal scheduled production line, not hand-made for the demo.
- [ ] Extends the invariant suite; never weakens it. Any invariant change names the invariant and cites the authorising decision ticket.
- [ ] Declares its depth grade as a **computed checklist** against the owning decision ticket's acceptance criteria — `full` is derived from the checklist, never asserted.
