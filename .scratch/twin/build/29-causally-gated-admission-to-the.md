# 29 — Causally-gated admission to the £

**What to build:** The pricing boundary is **derived, not declared**. An impact enters the £ only if a path from the
component to cash flow exists at an adequate evidence grade. Reputation and morale price via churn,
attrition and grievance paths — or they do not price at all.

This is what stops the £ becoming a place where anything can be asserted to be worth something.

**Blocked by:** 28, 19

**Status:** ready-for-agent
