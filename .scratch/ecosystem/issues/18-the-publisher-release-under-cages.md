❓ **Q1** - **What a degraded publish looks like on the wire**: the declared bump is weaker than computed, the number cannot be rewritten (ADR-0011), and adopters never see `policy/v*` tags (fact above). ➡️ (a). The version string is the only thing every consumer reads and it should say what happened (ticket 06 research rule 12, a recommendation not a decision); a suffix keeps the declared base number and its ordering below the clean number is the point.
