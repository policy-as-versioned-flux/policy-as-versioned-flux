# 14 — Insurance and the insurer party

Type: grilling (HITL)
Status: prepared
Blocked by: 01, 07

## Question

The risk-transfer half. Each org's appetite object gains an attachment point, a limit and an exclusion list. Transfer is priced off TVaR between attachment and limit. The insurer party publishes a signed quote artefact against those terms; what the quote contains, how it is versioned, how an adopter subscribes to it, and how the balance sheet shows retained versus transferred.

## Notes

Reversal 20. Blocked by the orgs existing and by size/obligations being declared.

## Grilling round 1 — drafted 2026-08-28, HELD

Drafted AFK on the owner's instruction. Adversarially checked by two reviewers. Nothing below has been put to the owner.

❓ **Q1** - **What the cover terms are keyed on, and the attachment's grain**: `appetite` on `party.yaml` gains `attachment`, `limit` and `exclusions` (settled). Two things are open. Exclusions: (a) keyed on the ticket 07 obligation regime names and the re-grill 19 `(source, id)` control keys, as `exclusions: [{kind: obligation|control, source?, id}]`; (b) free strings; (c) portfolio risk ids as in `driftwood-portfolio.json`. Attachment grain: today's only retention is a per-occurrence `deductible` (`tcor.py:93`), while `tolerance` is annual aggregate (CONTEXT.md:297); (i) attachment and limit are annual aggregate, so `attachment` is the retention `tolerance` already names and the two cannot disagree; (ii) per-occurrence, which needs a per-event loss list `fair.py` does not produce.

➡️ (a) and (i). Keying on regimes and `(source, id)` reuses the keys the composition already prices on, so an excluded item is priced as retained with no new lookup; (b) cannot be priced and (c) keys on an unsigned platform-local file. Aggregate is the only grain `fair.simulate()` can slice today (one annual list, `fair.py:60-88`) and it makes attachment and tolerance one number seen from two sides. Per-occurrence terms are fog until a carrier asks for them. Blocked by the schema PR: ticket 25 adds `appetite`; `size`/`obligations` have no build ticket yet, and this ticket's build needs both.

❓ **Q2** - **What the adopter publishes as its priced exposure**: the north star says the insurer consumes the adopter's priced exposure. Nothing publishable exists: `prices[]` entries are scalars with no distribution (`composition.py:1010-1018`), the only portfolio is platform-local, driftwood-only and unsigned by driftwood (H3-16), and forward-intel is one scenario. A layer between attachment and limit needs a distribution. Options: (a) the adopter's composed artefact gains an `exposure` section under `perspective: <adopter>`: the aggregate annual loss summary (`ale, var95, tvar, tail`, the `lm` spec or a loss-exceedance curve in the `twin/severity.py:190-206` shape), computed by summing the per-risk annual loss lists year by year (independent frequency draws across risks, correlated within a breach as `fair.py:64-76` already does), a new aggregate `fair.py` does not have; (b) the insurer re-simulates from the adopter's pinned inputs (regime, size, forward-intel) and publishes nothing new; (c) the portfolio file moves into the adopter repo and is signed there, and the insurer reads the raw triples.

➡️ (a). One engine, one signed input, and Q3's `priced_against` can then name it. The year-by-year sum is the smallest new arithmetic that yields a portfolio distribution, and the independence-across-risks choice is stated in the artefact rather than hidden. (b) makes the insurer run the adopter's model and the quote unattributable to a signed exposure; (c) publishes raw triples, which are inputs, not a priced exposure. Consequence, recorded not asked: the per-risk crossover loses its `transfer` move (a layer attaches to the aggregate, not to one risk), so `applicable: ["transfer"]` risks become "excluded or covered" at the layer, `ORDER` becomes fix / cage / the bottom rung (`deny` today, `isolated` if ticket 09 Q3 lands), and the war-gamer's `deployed_move == transfer` assertion (`wargamer.py:284-294`) is rewritten against the layer.

❓ **Q3** - **What the signed quote contains and how the premium enters the adopter's sheet**: the insurer's pricing is under `perspective: insurer`; ADR-0021 forbids any sum crossing perspectives, and the adopter's TCoR must include the premium. Options for the payload: (a) `{insured, terms as quoted (attachment, limit, exclusions, plus any the insurer adds), premium {amount, currency}, valid_from, valid_until, priced_against: {party artefact tag, composed artefact sha, exposure hash, forward-intel version}, conditions: [{source, id, consequence: void|uplift {amount, currency}}]}`; (b) minimal: insured, terms, premium, validity, no binding and no conditions; (c) (a) plus the insurer's full loss model so the adopter can recompute the premium. Premium booking: (i) the premium is a contract amount, a cost like `costs.fix`, booked under `perspective: <adopter>`; the insurer's TVaR arithmetic stays under `perspective: insurer` and is never summed with anything of the adopter's; (ii) the premium carries `perspective: insurer` and the adopter's sheet shows it as a separate, unsummed line.

➡️ (a) and (i). `priced_against` reuses ADR-0012's pinned-sha mechanism so a quote priced against a stale exposure is a visible fact and a Renovate bump is meaningful; principle 6 covers only the signature. `conditions` keyed on `(source, id)` with a declared `consequence` are the vocabulary research 07 §4.2 shows carriers use, and let the composition price a breached condition as whatever the signed quote says (void: the layer prices as fully retained; uplift: premium plus the stated amount) under ADR-0020 and re-grill 20, without this ticket inventing the rule. (i) is the only reading under which TCoR = retained layer + cost of controls + premium is a legal sum: the premium is what the adopter pays, not a Price in CONTEXT.md's sense. (ii) leaves TCoR without its largest new line. (c) publishes the insurer's book.

❓ **Q4** - **How quotes are laid out in the insurer repo**: ADR-0019 makes a release one gitsign-signed semver tag on the publisher repo, so one tag versions everything in the repo. `publishes[]` rows are `{kind, name, path, payload_schema}` (ticket 04 Answer 4). Options: (a) one feed `name: quote`, payload keyed by insured party, every tag re-prices every adopter; (b) one feed name per adopter (`quote-driftwood`, `quote-tuppence`, `quote-ludlow`), each its own `path` and `publishes[]` row, one shared `payload_schema`, one tag line; (c) one repo per adopter under the insurer org.

➡️ (b). The adopter's `inherits[]` entry then names exactly its own cover, and a tag that re-priced only another adopter lands as an honest `changed: false` line in `prices[]`. (a) makes every pin cover a payload mostly about other parties; (c) multiplies Renovate installs and `party.yaml` files for three adopters. (b) relies on the composer resolving `name` to `path` through `publishes[]`, which ticket 21 builds, so this ticket is blocked by 21 as well as 01, 07 and 25. Expiry is settled by derivation above.

❓ **Q5** - **Where the pricing run lives**: engine (fair.py), clock-not-skill and a human merge are settled. The open decision is the seat. Options: (a) the insurer repo pins `platform` and the adopter's exposure artefact through `inherits[]`, the way `driftwood/renovate.json:27-35` pins platform, and a scheduled workflow there runs `fair.py`'s layer maths under `perspective: insurer` over the pinned exposure with the insurer's own signed `load` and `conditions` files, opens a PR, a human merges and tags; (b) the platform repo prices quotes on the insurer's behalf and the insurer repo only signs what platform computed; (c) the insurer ships its own pricer.

➡️ (a). Independence of view comes from the insurer's own signed inputs, not from different arithmetic, and the truth surface gets one script in one repo to grade. A `publisher` that pins parents needs `inherits[]` on a publisher `party.yaml`, which ADR-0019 allows and no publisher does today; that is a schema fact for ticket 21, not a new mechanism. (b) puts the counterparty's price under the platform's tag, which makes the quote unattributable. (c) is a second engine on cost and truth-surface grounds: two verify scripts for one number and no way to say why they differ.

Later rounds, blocked on the above: aggregate versus per-obligation sub-limits (GDPR vs HIPAA), after Q1; which adopter carries the thin-slice quote first and whether all three need one before NORTH-STAR §4 step 4 ("the residual is on the balance sheet"), after Q4; the insurer's own `party.yaml` beyond `roles: [publisher]`: its size and whether it is priced as a risk-bearer by the same engine the way `platform` is, after Q5; the re-quote trigger (adopter exposure tag moved, forward-intel moved, calendar), which ticket 10 does not list today and should gain as a clock candidate; the verify script's shape (retained + transferred + excluded == simulated total; premium from the pinned quote under the adopter's perspective; expiry priced not refused; a breached condition priced per its declared consequence) and its wiring into `verify-all.sh`, after Q3; ticket 15 interplay: whether a priced hole and an excluded item are one "retained" line or two on the sheet.
