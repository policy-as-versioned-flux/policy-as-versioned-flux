## Grilling round 1 — drafted 2026-08-28, HELD

Drafted AFK on the owner's instruction. Adversarially checked by two reviewers. Nothing below has been put to the owner. Five questions.

❓ **Q1** - **What prices one hole, and whether it partitions or adds**: nothing joins a control id to a regime. Under ticket 07 each adopter has one sized triple per declared obligation, and `prices[0]` already carries the whole uk-gdpr exposure regardless of holes. Options: (a) uniform share: a hole prices at the regime exposure x (holes / selected controls), no new artefact, but MODERATE -> HIGH moves coverage 285/287 to 368/370, so the widening beat barely moves the pound; (b) regulator-published weights: the pricing parent (ico) adds, per violation type, the catalogue control ids it turns on, keyed `(source, id)`, and pins `nist` as a `controls` parent at a version so a catalogue bump cannot orphan the weights; the weights are a `payload_schema` change, so a major of the penalty feed under ADR-0019; a hole prices as weight x the adopter's sized triple for that regime; a control two obligations name prices under each and folds additively and correlated on the one incident, as `fair.py` already does for `--also`; (c) adopter-declared scenario per hole in its overlay, reusing the restate scenario mechanism, which is 285 declarations nobody will sign. In every option the regime entry becomes the sum of its weighted holes, so implementing a control reduces it; a hole is a partition of the regime exposure, never an addition on top.

➡️ (b). It is the only option under which reversal 10's widening beat and NORTH-STAR step 2 both happen, and it puts the join where the eco-system says it belongs: the regulator, as a signed feed version. The partition reading is what makes "the pound moves visibly" true; the additive reading would count the same money twice against `prices[0]`. (a) is a count wearing a price.

❓ **Q2** - **Where the hole price lands and which refusals go**: three refusals stand today: new hole, baseline widening, new ungoverned namespace (`composition.py:880-947, 516-530`). ADR-0020 and reversals 9-10 leave none of them. Options: (a) the regime `prices[]` entry gains a per-hole breakdown and a total under the adopter's perspective; the selection policy reads the entry it already reads, so holes move the tier; all three refusals are deleted and a new or widened hole and a new ungoverned namespace print as priced deltas; (b) onto the balance sheet line only, no effect on the tier, all three refusals deleted. Dependency either way: two of three adopters already sit at `deny` on both entries, so the beat is observable only on driftwood until ticket 09's `isolated` rung lands and ticket 25 moves appetite onto `party.yaml`.

➡️ (a). NORTH-STAR step 3 is "the pound crosses a band, the cage tier moves"; a price that cannot move the tier is a report, not a cage. A new ungoverned namespace is a missing behaviour under ADR-0020: priced from the day it appears, never refused, which is CONTEXT.md's current wording already. The ticket's Question line "a £ refusal threshold" is reworded on resolution.

❓ **Q3** - **How an ungoverned namespace's price grows**: re-grill 27 says priced, proportionate to the workloads inside, growing over time. The walk is repo-only and a re-ungoverned namespace is `new` with no history. Options: (a) share of the adopter's uncaged residual, workloads in the namespace over workloads in all institution namespaces in the same repo walk, with LEF ramped past `since` by the same ramp the EOL feed already uses, bounded at the whole residual; `since` is the tag date of the first signed composed artefact that recorded the name (ADR-0012), no new header field; (b) flat share with the `since` date printed beside it, which is the grandfather clause re-grill 27 rejected.

➡️ (a). Both factors are things the composition already reads and the ramp already exists, so no invented formula and no knob; the bound keeps it a price of a share. The proposer's `governed: "true"` PR (reversal 18) then has a price to cite. Re-opened after close must keep its original `since`, so recorded status survives a close.

❓ **Q4** - **Where the unpriced register's cage consequence is attached, and to which rung**: re-grills 32 and 35 give every register entry a cage consequence from appetite. ADR-0021 says the twin emits a scenario and never a tier and the payload has no register field, so the twin cannot stamp a rung and the estate cannot see the entries. The consequence is a tier from ticket 09's value space, not a twin observe/warn/constrain/block rung (GAPS 3.8). Options: (a) the forward-intel payload gains `register[]` of unpriced hits (a `payload_schema` change, major, built in ticket 25); the estate's selection policy gives each entry the tier it selected for the priced hits in the same scenario under the same perspective, strictest if nothing in the scenario priced; (b) same payload change, every register entry defaults to strictest, mirroring reversal 17's unknown-tier rule.

➡️ (a). The rung comes from the appetite-selected tier with no new knob and no invented figure, the estate side keeps ADR-0021's seam, and nothing escapes: connected to a priced hit through an unpriced hop runs no looser than the hit. (b) is honest but puts most of the map in the tightest cage. An adopter-declared `unpriced_default_tier` is not offered: it is a second appetite object ticket 08 ruled out.

❓ **Q5** - **Where a bespoke control is published, and who prices it**: re-grill 25 admits namespaced ids as priced holes. A component-definition claims against a catalogue named by its `source` href; it cannot define a control, and `_catalog_ids` reads one nist root. Options: (a) the adopter ships a small OSCAL catalogue as a `controls` parent of itself (ESLint self-versioning), `source: <adopter>`, and the composition resolves `(source, id)` across every `controls` parent; (b) a `controls:` list on `party.yaml`, the shape ADR-0017 rejected for claims as a second format. Price, in either: an adopter-signed scenario per bespoke control, the restate `_load_scenario` mechanism (`composition.py:595`). Sub-option for a bespoke control with no scenario: (i) instrument fault, refuses (ADR-0020's instrument line); (ii) priced at the regime's mean per-control weight from Q1.

➡️ (a) with (i). The party that invented the control is the only party that can say what missing it costs, and a catalogue it publishes is the same object every other publisher ships. (ii) launders a regulator's weight onto a control the regulator never named. Regulator weights under Q1(b) never name a bespoke id, so no double count.

Later rounds, blocked on the above: a baseline control no regime weight names, priced at zero or an instrument fault (Q1); which baseline each adopter selects once selection is visibly priced, e.g. ludlow on HIGH for HIPAA (Q1, Q2); how a hole reads under a non-adopter perspective (Q2, ticket 08 labelling, ticket 11); the party schema change that admits `overlay.controls` and the `since` on `inherits[]` (ticket 04) as the namespace's date source (Q3); whose appetite the twin runs under for the regulator's or insurer's perspective (Q4, ticket 11); superseding notes for ADR-0013 (bare-id key, new-hole refusal, widening refusal), ADR-0017 (self-created hole refuses), ADR-0018 point 3 (new-ungoverned refusal) and ADR-0015 (deny-to-issue, with ticket 09); how a priced hole shows on the `tcor.balance_sheet` line once its appetite source moves (Q2, ticket 25); whether "driven by its regulators" means derived `baseline:` rather than visibly priced selection (reversal 9 reading).
