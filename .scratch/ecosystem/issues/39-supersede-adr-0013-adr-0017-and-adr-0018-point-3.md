**Decisions** (all delegated, ADR-0025):

- D1 `(source, id)` is a refinement of ADR-0013's bare-id rule, not a replacement. Reason: ticket 15 re-grill 19 says the wire value stays the bare catalogue id and the claim key becomes `(source, id)`; ADR-0013 already named the catalogue once by the enclosing href, so the source was always half the key, and ticket 38 D2 keeps the three real adopters' headers byte-stable. What is superseded is the one-catalogue assumption, which ticket 15 item 5's adopter self-catalogue breaks.
- D2 The removal refusal is retired: a removal is priced, never refused, as a `removed-control` delta with the amount the hole carried, status `unselected` on the regime line with its amount unchanged, and one `baseline-narrowing` summary delta for a named-baseline change that drops controls, mirroring the widening delta. Reasons, in the ADR's order: the doctrine admits one refusal and it is a missing instrument, and a removed control is a missing behaviour (ticket 38's own framing); ADR-0013's reason ("an exemption by another name") described a count a removal could hide a hole from, and under pricing the regulator's weight prices a control whether or not the adopter selected it (`_decorate_regime_holes`: `unselected` keeps its amount, the partition still sums to the entry, graded by pound-seam), so a removal hides nothing from the pound; ADR-0022 already settled the same shape one layer down ("lowering the floor is priced, never refused"); the one pound a removal withdraws is a bespoke control's own scenario, an obligation the adopter invented and signs the withdrawal of, and the insurer's quote conditions (ticket 14) are where that has consequences. The ratchet alternative (union with the last signed set) was rejected as duplicated state under ADR-0018 §2 and as a count that never falls. Ticket 38 D6 is superseded by this.
