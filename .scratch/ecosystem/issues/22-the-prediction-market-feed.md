## Grilling round 1 — drafted 2026-08-28, HELD

Drafted AFK on the owner's instruction. Adversarially checked by two reviewers. Nothing below has been put to the owner. Five questions. The first draft's "which £ input a move may touch" question was found to be settled by reversal 6 and ADR-0021 and is recorded above, not asked.

❓ **Q1** - **Which markets the feed carries, authored or mechanical**: the feed needs a universe. Only the sanctions family is liquidly covered and ~91% of venue volume is sports/politics/crypto. The twin already owns one versioned mechanical rule (`benchmark-selection-rule.yaml`) and chose it over a curated list because any relevance filter is a selection lever. Binding a market to a scenario family is twin-side judgement (the skill, Q4), so no option below names one. Options: (a) a versioned, signed, mechanical rule file in the feeds repo, the benchmark-rule shape (category list, liquidity floor, horizon window, seeded volume valve) applied to everything the venue lists; a rule change is a dated diff and a minor bump; (b) an authored, versioned, signed watchlist of venue market ids in the feeds repo; adding an id is a minor bump; (c) everything above a liquidity floor, no category filter; the skill sorts it out.

➡️ (a). It is the discipline twin ticket 21 Q2 already chose and the same file shape, so the feed reproduces from rule plus venue corpus (re-grill 5) and the benchmark series is carried by construction, which is what reversal 22's scorer will need. (b) is the authored option twin Q2 rejected: a hand-picked list is a relevance lever with no rule to point at. (c) fetches the sports and crypto volume the category list exists to drop. A watchlist as a narrowing on top of the rule is fog until the rule selects too much.

❓ **Q2** - **Single venue or corroborated from version 1**: the three venues are decided (Kalshi, Polymarket, Metaculus). Polymarket is the fact-backed first adapter: no credential, a listing API (Gamma) and a price-history API, the highest rate limits. Research wanted two-venue corroboration; the map wants the thinnest slice first. A `venue` field on every observation makes a second venue a minor bump. Options: (a) version 1 reads Polymarket only; Kalshi lands as a minor bump with the same rule applied to its listing; (b) version 1 requires both Polymarket and Kalshi and carries a per-market `corroborated` flag where both list the same question; (c) as (b) plus Metaculus for long horizons (an API key held as a repo secret, and its ToS read).

➡️ (a). The thin slice needs one adapter on a clock, and a single-venue series is honest as long as `venue` is on every observation; corroboration is a consumer-side reading over two venues' series, not a publisher claim, and it arrives with (b) as a minor bump under ADR-0019. (c) adds a secret and a ToS question for a horizon band the research found thin. Polymarket's redistribution terms must be read before the first tag (see Later).

❓ **Q3** - **The payload, series or series plus moves**: the ticket names market id, question, dated price series and source. A feed version is a snapshot; the consumer diffs pins. The twin derives moves itself with `price_moves()` on its own schedule; the estate never prices a move, so no estate-side consumer needs one. The invariant forbids any probability-shaped field. Options: (a) dated series only, per market `{market_id, venue, question, resolution_source, observations[{date, price_level}]}`; (b) series plus a derived `moves[]` block (from/to date and level, delta, direction) in the same payload. `resolution_source` is an addition beyond the ticket text: the research grades evidence by resolution source, not venue (`prediction-markets.md:756-758`).

➡️ (a). It is the smallest schema with nothing derived to keep consistent; `price_level` travels with the bias citation the invariant already checks; the only consumer that wants moves (the twin) already derives them; (b) doubles the surface a `payload_schema` major would cover and publishes a derivative the publisher would then have to keep in step with the series.

❓ **Q4** - **The skill a human runs over a fetched version**: reasoning is a Claude Code skill a human runs. The twin's `signal-classify` seam takes a bare callable and already merges world layer and overlay as binding candidates; an unbound move decays under `decay.yaml`. Options: (a) the market-moves skill is the twin's `signal-classify` body: the human runs it over the moves the twin derived from the pinned feed, its output is the existing `market-signal-run` artefact, and bind, decay and forward-intel follow unchanged; (b) a new estate-side skill that writes a signed artefact the composition reads.

➡️ (a). The seam, the artefact, the quarantine exclusion and the decay all exist on the twin side, and the settled £ path runs through the twin's forward-intel anyway; (b) builds a second judgement artefact on the side of the seam that never prices a move. Where the artefact lands so the next scheduled run consumes it is ticket 10's shape.

❓ **Q5** - **Cadence, trigger and how the bump is declared**: ticket 04 Answer 3 says the publisher declares the bump in a versioned file reviewed in a PR; re-grill 8 allows a feed a computed bump. Once ticket 21 adds a Renovate customManager over `inherits[]` (not yet written), every tag opens a PR in each subscriber. A daily tag on every tick floods subscribers; a tag only on a large move hides the series. Options: (a) a daily LLM-free fetch appends observations and opens a publisher PR every run declaring a patch; a human merges; the workflow tags on merge; (b) a daily fetch appends observations to a branch every run, but opens the publisher PR (dedupe by branch) only when some selected market moved by at least a threshold declared in the Q1 rule file (e.g. 5 percentage points) since the last tag; the PR declares patch for observations, minor for a rule change, major for a schema change; a human merges; the workflow tags on merge; (c) the workflow computes the bump and tags on the same threshold with no PR between fetch and tag.

➡️ (b). The feed is named for the move, so the move is the release trigger; the threshold is a versioned number in the rule file, not a judgement per run; a human still reviews and merges every release, which keeps ticket 04's declared-bump shape and re-grill 29's separate identities; subscribers get a PR only when the world's assessment changed, which is what NORTH-STAR principle 5 means by re-pricing on a clock. (a) is a daily review of a data append. (c) drops the reviewed PR from the publisher side. Cadence is decided here as daily; ticket 10 consumes this trigger and owns dedupe and rejection half-life across all scheduled steps.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-08-28.

Owner record. On 2026-08-28 the owner read the held round and wrote: "ive already read the recommendations and I can't find fault with a single one. Well done. Get everything ready for me to then to-spec". Under the map's process rule a bare agree does not ratify. The five decisions below are PROVISIONAL, recorded with that line verbatim and each recommendation's own reason as the rationale, as tickets 04, 07 and 08 were. The five-per-day rule was overridden by the owner's instruction. Conflict C3 was then put to the owner as decision D2 with a three-lens panel verdict. The owner wrote: "I agree with you're more advanced reasoning". D2 is DECIDED with that line verbatim.

1. Universe. The feed carries every market the venue lists that passes a versioned, signed, mechanical rule file in the feeds repo. The file has the benchmark-rule shape: category list, liquidity floor, horizon window, seeded volume valve. A rule change is a dated diff and a minor bump. Rationale: twin ticket 21 Q2 chose this discipline because any relevance filter is a selection lever. The feed reproduces from rule plus venue corpus (re-grill 5). The benchmark series is carried by construction. Amendment D2: the same rule file also defines "changed" for this feed (item 5).

2. Venue. Version 1 reads Polymarket only. Every observation carries `venue`. Kalshi lands as a minor bump under the same rule. Rationale: one adapter on a clock is the thin slice. Corroboration is a consumer reading over two series, not a publisher claim. Polymarket's redistribution terms are read before the first tag.

3. Payload. Dated series only. Per market: `{market_id, venue, question, resolution_source, observations[{date, price_level}]}`. No `moves[]` block. No probability-shaped field. Rationale: the smallest schema, with nothing derived to keep in step. The twin derives moves with `price_moves()`. `resolution_source` is the evidence grade the research names.

4. Skill. The market-moves skill is the twin's `signal-classify` body. A human runs it over the moves the twin derived from the pinned feed. Output is the existing `market-signal-run` artefact. Bind, decay and forward-intel follow unchanged. Rationale: the seam, the artefact, the quarantine exclusion and the decay exist on the twin side, and the settled £ path runs through forward-intel. C4 stands: reasoning is a human-run skill. Where the artefact lands is ticket 10.

5. Cadence and trigger. Daily LLM-free fetch. Amended by D2: this is an instance of ticket 10's one rule, not a rule of its own. The scheduled fetch appends observations to the feed's observation branch on every run. It opens the publisher PR only when the computed bump is not `none`. "Changed" for this feed (a selected market moved at least the threshold since the last tag, for example 5 percentage points) lives in the versioned rule file beside the feed, not in `payload_schema`. The PR declares patch for observations, minor for a rule change, major for a schema change. A human merges. The workflow tags on merge. D1 applies: the clock appends observations, never a declaration. Rationale: the feed is named for the move, so the move is the release trigger. The reviewed PR keeps ticket 04's declared bump and re-grill 29's separate identities. The daily cadence and the reviewed-PR shape are PROVISIONAL (Q5(b)); the D2 amendment is DECIDED, rationale: one rule owned by ticket 10, each feed defining "changed" in its own versioned rule file, so sub-threshold observations still append and a series survives.
