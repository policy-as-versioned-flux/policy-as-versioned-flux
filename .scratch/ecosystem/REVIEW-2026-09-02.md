### R1. The £ inputs are editorial, two are stale, and the ladder saturates — CRITICAL

This is the finding the last review missed and the one that decides the demonstration.

Every number that turns a severity into money is an authored constant. The frequency that annualises the largest line is `DEFAULT_WARN_LEF = (1, 2, 4)`, which asserts about 2.2 ICO monetary penalties a year against one retailer and cannot draw a loss-free year (`pound-engine/PE-02`). The tier reduction table is `0.30 / 0.70 / 0.92 / 0.98`, self-declared by the platform, and driftwood's own published response curve disagrees with it (`principles/P3-1`). The threat line's loss magnitude is a platform fixture keyed by adopter name, the exact class ADR-0021 retired (`pound-engine/PE-07`). The control weights that apportion the regulator's exposure are round tenths with no stated basis (`PE-10`). The insurer prices its layer at one twenty-second of the expected loss on that layer, an implied loss ratio of 2,179 percent (`PE-01`), from an exposure pinned to a tag whose tree has no exposure section (`participants/P1`, `PE-11`).

Two of the five real fines the ICO schema cites are no longer what it says. Doorstep Dispensaree's £275,000 was reduced to £92,000 by the First-tier Tribunal in 2021 and confirmed at that figure by the Court of Appeal in 2024. Clearview AI's £7,552,800 was set aside in 2023, reinstated on jurisdiction by the Upper Tribunal in October 2025, remitted for the substantive appeal, and has never been collected. The completeness critic ran the real production path with the corrected inputs. Dropping Clearview as not final moves driftwood's largest signed line from £1,787,177 to £656,932, a fall of 63 percent, and the insurer's signed premium from £113,403 to £63,955. The payload schema has no field for litigation status. Sources: [Hunton](https://www.huntonak.com/privacy-and-information-security-law/uk-first-tier-tribunal-cuts-icos-doorstep-dispensaree-fine-by-two-thirds), [DataGuidance](https://www.dataguidance.com/news/uk-court-appeal-dismisses-doorstep-dispensarees-appeal), [ICO on Clearview](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2025/10/uk-upper-tribunal-hands-down-judgment-on-clearview-ai-inc/), [Ashfords on permission to appeal](https://www.ashfords.co.uk/insights/articles/the-extra-territorial-reach-of-the-uk-gdpr-clearview-ai-is-permitted-to-appeal).

Only driftwood declares a size. Tuppence and ludlow are priced at the statutory cap, £9,039,791 each, identical to the last decimal, with every per-customer figure null (`PE-08`, `scope/F2`). No adopter declares obligations. The FCA and HIPAA converters that ticket 24 decided do not exist. The US health provider is priced by the UK ICO schema and nothing else.

The consequence I found myself and the skeptics confirmed: all three governed namespaces declare `isolated`, the rung the north star itself calls "too expensive to run or not functional". Tuppence and ludlow fit no rung at all and fail closed. Driftwood's two large lines have 5 and 12 percent of headroom before they also fail off the end. The demonstration therefore begins where §4 step 4's "caged tighter" has nowhere to go. The only real Renovate bump that ever merged left the price unchanged (`demo-steps/DS-F3`). Nothing sums what an adopter retains, so every line reads inside the band while driftwood carries 2.2 times it (`PE-05`).

The engine is not the problem. Three published numbers reproduce by hand to the last digit. The problem is what the estate says the number is. Its own code prints qualifiers its artefacts omit.

## 4. Where this review disagreed with itself, and how I ruled

The adversarial pass produced contradictions. A synthesis that hides them is worth less than one that rules on them.

1. **Saturation.** `scope/F1` claimed step 3 is arithmetically foreclosed; the skeptic refuted "cannot" and confirmed "has not". I accept the refutation and keep the narrower fact, which the skeptic strengthened into R2. My own pre-workflow arithmetic made the same overreach; the skeptic was right.
2. **The cert skew.** `security/SS-01` was refuted on ownership only: ticket 73 owns it. The defect is real and structural. Across the seven newest signed tags, the certificate's notBefore is one second after the tagger time in three, because git writes the tag before Fulcio issues the certificate. A verifier that chains at tagger time will reject about half of correctly signed tags by construction. Ticket 73's "clock skew" framing understates it. Recorded on 73.
3. **Instrument or estate.** `demo-steps/DS-F5` called the step-4 red an instrument fault and was refuted. The failing component is platform's shipped source-boundary controller, which suspends real Kustomizations. The gate reported it correctly. Estate fault.
4. **The unpinned Flux install.** `truth-surface/TS-M3` was refuted for attributing a lost run to it; `security/SS-06` survived on the root-before-signing mechanism. Both are right. The line is real, unowned, contradicts the estate's own rule in three unit workflows, and installs a tool no verify script calls. Minor as reproducibility, major as supply chain. In 77.
5. **At least three versions.** Six findings split three ways. The requirement's source could not be looked at. Recorded as Q3, and the one-line fact stays major, not critical, until the author says three binds.
6. **The clock has no cluster.** Graded critical, major and not-a-finding on identical evidence. Ruled in R5: the fact is a decision; the unciteability of the cage is the finding.
7. **Step 2 done?** The thesis auditor lists PR #20 as a strength; the demo auditor finds the price did not move and the e2e wording says "merged" about a simulation. Both true. The bump is real; the re-price is not. Q2.
8. **The currency controller.** One auditor says withdraw the retirement, another says delete the ballast. The recorded reason is a category error either way. The owner re-decides; folded into 75 as a small question.
9. **Twin pin.** `TWIN-08` was refuted as overstated while three siblings survived on the same fact. The surviving statement is the narrow one: the feed is outside every tag and its absence is silent. Ticket 64.
10. **Copy-pasted adopters.** `EQ-03` refuted on framing, `O7` survived. The measurement stands: 15 of 39 shared files identical, 24 diverged, including the five-fact grader. Whether that is a defect is Q7.

Some assessment files were written before their own refutations landed and still carry withdrawn sentences in their verdict paragraphs. Read the surviving list, not the verdict paragraph, in each `assess/*.md`.
