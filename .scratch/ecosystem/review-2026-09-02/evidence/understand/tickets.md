# Map of the 74 eco-system tickets

Source: `.scratch/ecosystem/issues/01..74`, `.scratch/ecosystem/map.md`. All quotes/paraphrases are
from the ticket files themselves (path: `.scratch/ecosystem/issues/<file>.md`); "map.md" refers to
`.scratch/ecosystem/map.md`. Claim-type legend: **Decision** = a grilling/research verdict with no
build; **Build/citable** = something built whose Answer names a check *and* cites a TRUTH line that
independently exists; **Build/local** = something built but proven only by a local run, a hand-typed
table, or a TRUTH citation that turns out not to exist; **Assertion** = a factual claim (an org
created, a doc rewritten) with no gate check at all. Ratification legend: **bare agree** = owner
wrote "Agree"/"Lgtm"/the "can't find fault" line with no reason of their own; **reasoned yes** = the
owner gave an actual reason in their own words; **decided (D-item)** = one of the five cross-ticket
conflicts (D1–D5, panel verdict, owner: "I agree with you're more advanced reasoning"), which the
map and tickets record as the only non-provisional ratification besides research/task tickets where
ratification doesn't apply (N/A).
