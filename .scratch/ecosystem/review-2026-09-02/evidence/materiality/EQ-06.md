# Materiality judgement — EQ-06

Verdict: MATERIAL. Severity: major (not critical).

Reframed: The enactment half of the thesis — signed policy actually in force on a running
cluster — is permanently could-not-look on the only citable instrument, and the route that
was designed to fix it is built for one adopter beat and unowned for platform's ten.

## Why it is material

- NORTH-STAR §5: "One command, on a schedule, in CI, is the only source any document may cite
  for 'what works'". The citable instrument is CI-only (ticket 40's 2026-09-01 correction
  withdraws a figure because "the TRUTH line of 2026-08-29 was itself a local rehearsal"), and
  CI has no substrate: `.github/workflows/truth.yml` installs gitsign, kyverno, cosign and flux
  binaries only.
- The unlooked beats are precisely the runtime half the thesis insists on: the admission gate
  for the catastrophic minority (research/03-blogs-thesis.md:110-128, :195-200), principle 2
  (everything caged) and principle 7 (Flux integral unless disproven), and §4 steps 4-5.
  Run-21 captures on origin/main show the cluster SKIPs are `verify-access`, `verify-currency`,
  `verify-engine`, `verify-eud`, `verify-graded`, `verify-federation`, `verify-identity`,
  `verify-posture-projection`, `verify-source-verification`, `verify-declared-versions-admit`
  (platform) and `verify-reach-secrets` (tuppence) — the cage ladder, the identity spine and
  the source verification.
- It is NOT a recorded scope decision that no clock stands up a cluster. Ticket 16 Q3 decided
  the opposite and named the shape: "(a) a scheduled workflow in the adopter's own repo brings
  up an ephemeral KinD, installs the engine, reconciles from the real remotes with spec.verify
  on, records the Q1 sample and commits it to drift/samples.jsonl ... the hub's daily truth.yml
  stays the aggregate." So the design answer exists; it is half-built.

## Corrections to the finding's own framing (they cap it below critical)

- The instrument is honest here. Every cluster SKIP reads
  `SKIP: offline proof holds; live tail could not look: kind cluster 'driftwood' is not listed`
  — the offline half is graded PASS and the live half is a reasoned could-not-look, which is
  exactly the third outcome §5 requires. Principle 6 ("a green that could not look is a red")
  is not breached. So "a regression is undetectable" is true only for live-object state, not
  for the offline proof of the same beat.
- "No open ticket proposes provisioning a cluster on the clock" is wrong for the adopter half:
  driftwood's `.github/workflows/drift-sample.yml` IS a KinD-in-Actions clock (ticket 40),
  ticket 60 rewired `verify-reconcile.sh` and `verify-e2e-step4` to grade its lane sample
  cluster-free, and ticket 60 records that its first firing ever (2026-09-01 11:41Z) failed on
  `curl -o kind` and the fix waits on the owner's merge. Tickets 60 and 74 carry that thread.
- The genuinely unowned residue is platform's ten live tails plus tuppence's reach-and-secrets:
  no lane-sample path, no ephemeral-cluster clock, no open ticket. That is the finding's real
  core and it survives.
- 3 of the 18 SKIPs are the one-declared-version cause (coexistence, retirement, shift-left,
  publisher-gate part C), a different defect; do not bundle them here.

## Severity

Major, not critical. §4 already states "None of steps 1 to 5 runs end to end today", so no
document is claiming the ambition on the strength of these beats, and the SKIPs are honest.
But §5's stated principle — the truth surface is the only citable source of what works — is
not true of the runtime half of the estate: roughly a sixth of the denominator is structurally
ungradeable on the citable clock, with no owner for the platform share.
