## Correction 2 — CONTEXT.md discloses the shipped Deny; what it gets wrong is the supersession

CONTEXT.md:227-228 names the shipped form: "The shipped `Deny` form is the July record". So
"CONTEXT.md says it is not [a live Deny]" overstates, and so does "a reader of CONTEXT.md would
report principle 2 as satisfied". The real defect is the second half of that sentence:

- ADR-0022 does not supersede the orphan guard — it never mentions it.
- `docs/adr/0014-unclaimed-is-caged-governed-namespace-requires-claim.md:5` (the supersession
  banner) reads: "**Superseded in part, 2026-08-28.** the CREATE deny on a governed namespace is
  superseded by ADR-0022. **The rest stands.**" ADR-0014's body is the decision that the orphan
  guard's claim-deny is correct ("The code is right about the guard"), and the banner leaves it
  standing.

So CONTEXT.md asserts a supersession neither ADR makes, and no code change followed.
