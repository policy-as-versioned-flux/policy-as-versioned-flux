# PE-04 refutation pass — "appetite.tolerance is one signed number used as three different economic quantities"

Verdict: **REFUTED as stated.** The three code sites are real and I re-derived every one of them.
Two of the finding's four legs fail: the "different units of meaning" framing, and — decisively —
"Claimed ownership: none found ... no ticket names the conflation".

## What refutes the finding as written

**(a) "Claimed ownership: none found. ... no ticket names the conflation" is false.**

- `CONTEXT.md:308-312` — "**Appetite** (rewritten 2026-08-28, ticket 14) — The annual loss an adopter
  declares it will carry ... The £ selects the first cage tier whose residual sits under it. It also
  carries the adopter's cover terms, if any: **attachment and limit** with exclusions, **so the
  attachment equals the appetite** and the sheet shows retained, transferred and excluded loss
  summing to the simulated total."
  One written definition, and it explicitly binds readings (2) and (3) together.
- `.scratch/ecosystem/spec.md:101` — "As the insurer, I want exclusions keyed on regime names and
  control ids and **attachment equal to appetite** ..." and `spec.md:174` — "Attachment equals appetite."
- `.scratch/ecosystem/issues/14-insurance-and-the-insurer-party.md:46` (Q1) poses exactly the grain
  question the finding says nobody asked: "Attachment grain: today's only retention is a
  per-occurrence `deductible` (`tcor.py:93`), while `tolerance` is annual aggregate (CONTEXT.md:297);
  (i) attachment and limit are annual aggregate, so `attachment` is the retention `tolerance` already
  names and the two cannot disagree; (ii) per-occurrence ...".
  `:48` records the owner's decision with a reason: "➡️ (a) and (i) ... Aggregate is the only grain
  `fair.simulate()` can slice today ... and **it makes attachment and tolerance one number seen from
  two sides**." `:79` restates it as Answer 1.
- `issues/14:24` even lists the readers side by side: "`risk/enforce.py` decides on `ALE_warn -
  ALE_deny`; `graded/cage.py` selects on residual mean; `tcor.py` and the premium read ALE."
  (Framed there as ALE-vs-tail, not as an appetite-units question — so this leg is weaker than the
  three above, but it is not "none found".)

So the reading the finding says is most dangerous — "one of them is what the carrier attaches its
layer at" — is the one the owner explicitly asked about, explicitly chose, and wrote into CONTEXT.md,
spec.md and the ticket.

**(b) "three quantities with different units of meaning" overstates.**
All three comparands are annualised GBP under the adopter's own perspective. (2) and (3) are the same
*defined* quantity (retained annual loss); they differ in aggregation **scope** — (2) is a per-scenario
caged residual, (3) is the sum over priced regime edges (£3.70M for driftwood) — not in units.
That scope difference is a real observation, but it is a different, narrower finding than the one filed.

**(c) "Whichever reading the owner intended, the other two are wrong" is false.**
Two of the three are the documented intent (CONTEXT.md:309-312 names both the cage selection and the
attachment in one sentence).

**(d) Reading (1) is arithmetically ~the retained-loss test, not an alien quantity.**
`risk_bought = ALE_warn - ALE_deny`, and the estate's own gated fixture has `ALE_deny = £345.85`
against `ALE_warn = £54,866.16` — a 0.6% gap on a £40,000 band. The "deny" state is the closed path,
so `risk_bought ≈ ALE_warn` = the loss retained by not escalating. The framing differs from
CONTEXT.md's definition; the number barely does.

## What survives (narrower, and lower severity)

`enforce.decide` compares an **avoided-loss delta** to a band CONTEXT.md:308 defines as a **retained
annual loss**. That is one off-definition reading, not three. It is live and green in the gate
(`talk/captures/.estate-clone_platform_risk_verify-risk-tuned.out` on `origin/main`: "ok
driftwood(£40000 tol, from its OWN party.yaml): loose buys £19439 -> Audit | tightened buys £54520 ->
Deny ... PASS"). Its own docstring (`enforce.py:11`) still asserts "That verdict IS the Kyverno
`validationActions` value", which ADR-0022 superseded ("The £ selects `isolated` where it selected
`deny`"). `enforce.decide` is imported only by `platform/wargamer/wargamer.py:104-107` and
`platform/honesty/reflexive.py:62-64`; `composition.py:820,835` reaches `enforce` only for
`appetite_money`/`tolerance_for`, never for `decide`. So no served policy's `validationActions` is
computed from it — the residue is a stale demonstration/war-game lane, not the enactment path and not
the carrier's attachment.
