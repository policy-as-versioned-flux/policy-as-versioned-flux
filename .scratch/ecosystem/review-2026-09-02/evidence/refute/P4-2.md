   neither `priority` nor `preemptionPolicy` (`v1.1.0:composed/policies/v2.0.0/cage-tier.yaml:40`),

   admitted at all. The forgeable-tier hazard (reason 2) was observed only after the 2.0.2/3.0.1
   backports made pods creatable, and those trees were never cut (`versions.yaml:59-60`). The
   v1.1.0 composed tree also carries no `priorityclasses.yaml`. So what Flux reconciles is inert,
   not exploitable in place.
2. **"on an adopter" overstates the blast radius.** The composed set is opt-in
   (`composed-set.yaml:34-44`; it is not in `gitops/apps/kustomization.yaml`), and the only place
   it is applied is the ephemeral cluster the adopter's own workflow builds
   (`.github/workflows/drift-sample.yml:178`). No standing adopter cluster reconciles it. The
   ephemeral cluster is however the only in-force evidence the estate cites.
3. **"the green sample" is not green.** Run 21's driftwood sample FAILs fact 2
   (`talk/captures/.estate-clone_driftwood_verify-reconcile.out`, and the step-4 capture fails with
   it). The facts that are true — 3, 4 and 5, 16/16 objects, 19 inventory entries — are the ones
   proving the retired lines in force.
4. **The pod consequence is conditional.** `gitops/apps/pod.yaml` exists at neither v1.0.0 nor
