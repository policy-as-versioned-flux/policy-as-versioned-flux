## Correction 2 — the mechanism is misattributed to rounding

Rounding is not what removes the zero years. A beta-PERT on [1,4] has support starting at 1 (observed continuous minimum 1.0156), so no draw falls below 1 with or without `round()`. The lower bound of the editorial triple is the cause. Rounding in fact *creates* zeros wherever lo=0 — that is precisely how the deny state's 97% zero years arise.

The defensible statement of the modelling gap: fair.py uses the drawn rate directly as the event count, with no Poisson/Bernoulli count layer, so there is no zero-inflation anywhere. Counterfactual, same PERT rates: a Poisson count would give P(loss-free year) = E[e^−rate] = **0.132** for lef=(1,2,4) and **0.0213** for (2,4,9).
