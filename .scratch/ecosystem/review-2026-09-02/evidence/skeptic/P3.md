## Where the finding overstates

### A. "no pin of any kind — no tag" is false as written
The bare `version:` string is not inert. `verify/feed-contract/feed_contract.py:83-119`
resolves it against `git ls-remote --tags` on the publisher's **real remote**, and the
run-21 capture `talk/captures/verify_feed-contract_verify-feed-contract.out` PASSES it
for all three edges:
```
PASS: driftwood pins ico/feed/penalty-schema@v3: tag v3.0.0 on ico
PASS: driftwood pins feeds/feed/threat-register@v2: tag threat-register/v2.0.0 on feeds
PASS: driftwood pins insurer/feed/quote-driftwood@v1: tag v1.0.0 on insurer
```
(This is the check the previous review's M10 recorded as SKIPping in run 13; it now
passes.) Two real weakenings remain, and they are the honest version of the claim:
- the reference is a **floating major**. `feed_contract.py:64-71`: "A bare-major pin
  ('v1') resolves to ANY signed tag of that major (v1.x.y)". So it is not immutable.
- nothing binds the **consumed bytes** to that tag. The checkout is a branch; the
  `sha` that lands in `composed/HEADER.yaml` is derived after the fact by
  `_resolve_unpinned_sha` (`composition.py:405-435`) as `git log -1 -- <version dir>`
  on whatever tree was checked out. I reproduced it: `git -C units/ico log -1 --format=%H -- penalty-schema/v3`
  → `e1fb8eb5663e…`, exactly the value in `driftwood/composed/HEADER.yaml:19`. That is a
  record of what was read, not a constraint on what may be read. The function's own name
  says "unpinned".

So: **no commit pin and no Flux source — true. No tag — false.**

### B. "consumed by every adopter" is wrong for insurer
`units/tuppence/party.yaml` and `units/ludlow/party.yaml` `inherits[]` list only
platform, nist, ico and feeds. Neither declares an insurer edge, though both still
check the insurer repo out. Only driftwood inherits `insurer/feed/quote-driftwood`.

### C. The branch is `main` only for driftwood
tuppence and ludlow use `ref: main` for ico but `ref: ecosystem/thin-slice` for feeds
and insurer — 12 checkouts (`tuppence/.github/workflows/{shift-left.yml:360,367,
cut-release.yml:97,104, propose-tier.yml:182,189}` and the same six in ludlow). Per
ticket 62's own 2026-09-01 comment, that branch no longer exists on feeds, so those are
not "moving", they are dead: "tuppence and ludlow cannot propose, shift-left or cut a
release — their step-3 path is dead on the clock, not merely unsigned."

### D. "undercuts principle 4's 'bump only by reviewed PR'" — refuted for feeds and insurer
`units/driftwood/renovate.json` customManagers #3 and #4 track the feeds and insurer
`party.yaml` version lines against `git-tags` on the publishers' remotes, with a
`postUpgradeTasks` re-render of `composed/**`. It works: driftwood PR #20 "Update
dependency feeds/threat-register to v2" merged 2026-09-01T08:57:50Z. So the *declaration*
does bump by reviewed PR. What moves without a PR is the *content inside the pinned major
directory*, because the checkout is a branch. ico is the one edge with no bump surface at
all — the same file says so explicitly ("ico's own penalty-schema line stays un-renovated
on purpose"), and that comment is now stale, since ico does carry v1.0.0 and v3.0.0 tags.

### E. "three of five parent edges" is driftwood-scoped, and understates the estate
Estate-wide the feed-contract capture shows 18 declared parent edges (driftwood 5, ico 1,
insurer 4, ludlow 4, tuppence 4). Flux-pinned: 7 (three adopters × {platform, nist}, plus
insurer's platform pin). ico's own `nist/controls@1.1.0` edge and insurer's three
`exposure` edges are Flux-unpinned too. "Exactly two of five" is right for driftwood's
own party.yaml and only there.

### F. What the estate does verify, that the fit-impact wording elides
Signatures are checked — just not at consumption. Run 21 step 6 validated the Fulcio cert
subject and Rekor entry for ico v3.0.0 and insurer v1.0.0. The gap is that the tag whose
signature was verified is not the ref the composing job reads.
