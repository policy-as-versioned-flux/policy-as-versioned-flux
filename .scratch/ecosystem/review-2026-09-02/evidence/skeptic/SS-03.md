   `python3 -c "import party_artefact as p; print(p.validate_schema({...,'trust_domain':...,'bundle_endpoint':...}))"`

   Mechanism at `party/party_artefact.py:185-188` (`allowed_top = set(schema["properties"])`),
   schema `additionalProperties: False`, 9 allowed keys.

   none contains `trust_domain`, `bundle_endpoint` or `federates_with`. The peers' trust anchors

   `trustDomain: platform.acme.internal` / `bundleEndpointURL: https://spire.platform.acme.internal:8443`).
