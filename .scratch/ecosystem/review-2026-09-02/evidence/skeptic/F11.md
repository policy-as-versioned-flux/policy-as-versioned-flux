- **"between them they own one of the seven §4 steps" is false.** NORTH-STAR §2 assigns Platform

  distribution mechanism (Flux)" — that is §4 steps 2 (composition re-prices), 3 (£ crosses a
  band, cage tier moves) and 4 (Flux reconciles). The twin owns step 5. Four of seven, not one.
  The underlying assessment file (scope-and-coherence.md:299-302) makes the narrower and correct
  claim about the twin alone; the finding as circulated folds platform into it and misstates it.
- **"a green number would not evidence the north star's one sentence" does not follow.**
  `talk/verify-all.sh:88`: `[ "$fail" -eq 0 ] || exit 1`. The gate is a conjunction, and under
  `--live` a SKIP is a FAIL (`talk/verify-all.sh:16, 60-61`). A green therefore requires each of
  the seven §4 step checks to be observed-true. Share-of-checks cannot dilute a conjunction; extra
  platform checks add conditions, they do not average the north-star ones away.
- **"measures one participant's internals rather than the eco-system operating between
  participants" is overstated.** 26 of platform's 45 scripts name another unit; 13 assert against
  the live cluster, which is the shared enactment plane; and the hub's 16 checks are the dedicated
  cross-party proofs. What *is* true and sharper: only **2 of 45** platform scripts read a sibling
  unit's tree on disk (`compose/verify-composition.sh:25` → `$HERE/../../driftwood`;
  `distribution/verify-infra-declaration.sh`). The rest name adopters inside platform's own
  fixtures (e.g. `oscal/verify-upflow.sh:44` loads
  `../policy/scenarios/driftwood-root-residual.json`, a platform file).
