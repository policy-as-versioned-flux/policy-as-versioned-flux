## Hard-won content that lives only here (must be carried, in a sentence each)

Read from the glossary text and from `git log --format='%ad %h %s' -- CONTEXT.md` (54 commits; the lesson-bearing ones noted):

- **Reference workload** (CONTEXT.md:530-536; commit 0c1249d0 "Ticket 152: fact 7 gets a reference workload that the cage does not select"). A silent workload on the tightest rung reads as a cage that holds only if a reference workload outside the cage reaches what the first one must not. If the reference reaches nothing, the sample is "could-not-look", never a pass. This is exactly the target's bracketed network probe and NO MEASUREMENT, NO MOVE rule. The loop must carry it.
- **Nothing is denied; a workload that does not fit its cage does not run** (CONTEXT.md:90-100; ticket 89, commits cc0ce90d, 59de909b). The target's "Never a deny" on `serving-lost` restates it. Carry the sentence.
- **Orphan guard "has been wrong twice"** (CONTEXT.md:298-300; ticket 91, commit 4211105d): the record claimed isolation while the served body shipped `Deny`, and the gate graded the denial correct. Lesson: derive what you assert. The probe-not-label rule already encodes it; cite it in LOOP.md as the reason.
- **Unknown or unlabelled tier fails closed to `isolated`** (CONTEXT.md:94; ticket 119, commit 6ff9ef3b "an unlabelled Namespace is priced, and the cage stays put"). Needs a decision in the new loop: what a pod with no rung label is. Not covered in the target text.
- **Exemption banned outright** (CONTEXT.md:57-65; owner 2026-08-20, commits 7bb6824f, e7326506). SATURATED names "fix a control, insure, restate tolerance" as levers; none may become an exemption. Carry the ban as one line.
- **Floor** (CONTEXT.md:537-541) and **tighten-only** (CONTEXT.md:94-95): both contradicted by the target (loosening by release; every adopter's `floor: isolated` is what froze the old ratchet, `maps/A4:46`). These entries must be deleted, not carried; leaving them makes the glossary disagree with LOOP.md.
- **Exposure** entry (CONTEXT.md:634-637) means the figure an insurer quotes against; the target's Exposure is the probe-priced sum. Same word, two meanings: redefine.
- **Appetite** (CONTEXT.md:492-496): "the £ selects the first cage tier whose residual sits under it" is the one-lookup selection the target replaces. Replace by Tolerance. Note the premise in the question: the fresh Price entry (CONTEXT.md:485-490) does not contain the word "ordinal"; the ordinal ruling lives at `NORTH-STAR.md:122` and `docs/adr/0024:157,192`. Whoever edits must also supersede those, or the glossary and NORTH-STAR will disagree. Also "appetite" survives in `driftwood/party.yaml:4,52` as the block name (tolerance is a sub-field, line 60) and in `appetite.pricing_threshold` (CONTEXT.md:462), so a rename needs the party schema in step.
- **Rejection ledger** (CONTEXT.md:137-140): "declined proposal decays and re-raises" vs target "a closed one is a veto until an input changes". Different rule; state the new one.
