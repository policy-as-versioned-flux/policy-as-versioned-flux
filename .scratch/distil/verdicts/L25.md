# L25 verdict: nist and insurer

All paths are under `scratchpad/fresh/` (clones of 2026-11-14). Read-only pass.

## What the two repositories are

- nist: 12,655 lines, 68 files. 8,139 lines are `publisher-reliability/` (a monetary price-accuracy SDK and report). The rest is the real NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5.2.0 OSCAL catalogue (1,196 controls) plus LOW, MODERATE and HIGH baselines (`nist/README.md:21-40`, `nist/catalog/`). 40 commits. Newest work is 2026-10-18 and 2026-10-23: the monetary SDK, a shadow recovery-expense converter (`nist/publisher-reliability/price_*.py`).
- insurer: 4,167 lines, 34 files. `pricing/quote.py` (621 lines) prices a layer-rate premium per adopter. `quote/<adopter>/v1,v2/feed.json` are the published quotes. The attachment point is the adopter's own signed tolerance (`insurer/README.md:21-24`). New quotes carry `usage: illustrative` and say they are "model-only ordinal comparisons, not independent insurance or actuarial assurance" (`insurer/README.md:30-33`, `insurer/quote/driftwood/v2/feed.json:9`).

## Attempt 1: the loop runs without them. SUCCEEDS.

The target loop is: ico feed v5 (loss rows, penalty rate), feeds (threat rows with needs lists, rows outside the cage, exploited-flaw fetch), driftwood `party.yaml`, platform `loop.py` / `probe.py` / `inventory.py` / `rungs.yaml`.

1. Money. A4 row 22 and row 23 both say the tier does not move without them: "Lose rows 22, 23 and the premium line and the hole partition go. The tier does not move: the ICO line selects `isolated` with or without its partition" (`maps/A4-does-the-loop-run.md:221`). The composer states the same rule for the insurer: "`premium` is not exposure ... dropping an insurer moves the priceable exposure by nothing" (`platform/compose/composition.py:304-306`, `:533-535`). In the target the exposure sum is `events x (direct loss + penalty rate x penalty)` over rows with every need open. Nothing in that formula reads a premium or a control weight.
2. Controls. ICO's feed already carries its control ids as plain strings: `control_weights[regime][violation-type][] = {source: "nist", id: "sc-28", weight: 0.3, basis{kind: editorial ...}}` (`ico/penalty-schema/v4/feed.json:283-295`). The saturated message can print `id` and the editorial `basis` straight from the ico feed. No catalogue lookup is needed to print a string.
3. Insurance. The target names "insure" as a lever outside the cage and takes none. Naming a lever needs no insurer repository. The owner's own words are "put technological risk on the balance sheet of the business, be that for the biz value, insurance or other reasons" (2026-07-23, `maps/A1-owner-words.md:53`). That is a use of the figure, not a step in the loop. The insurer as a seventh party was the assistant's structure, off the back of an owner question: `maps/A1-owner-words.md:186`.
4. Imports. Platform's FAIR code (`platform/fair/fair.py`) does not import either repo. Only the old composer does: `composition.py:6033-6060` resolves the adopter's baseline against the nist tree (`_baseline_ids`) and refuses with `no-controls-parent` or `missing-baseline-file` without it; `:6574-6577` requires both `nist` and `insurer` parent trees. `platform/party/party_artefact.py:197-200` makes `baseline` mandatory for an adopter. All of that is the old chain, which the target parks at cut-over.
5. nist's monetary SDK is not the "rows for losses outside the cage" feed. `feeds/recovery-expense/README.md:3` says the family is `shadow-collection-only` and "Platform composition excludes this exact family from ordinary prices". It vendors nist schemas only as byte copies under `feeds/recovery-expense/contracts/` (`UPSTREAM.json`), so it does not read nist at run time. It belongs to the calibration programme, which the target already parks.
6. Schedules. nist runs a daily clock (`nist/.github/workflows/fetch.yml:32`, cron 02:41) and insurer one (`insurer/.github/workflows/fetch.yml:35`, cron 05:31). Both only propose pull requests or append observations. Stopping them changes no number the target prints.

## Attempt 2: the loop loses something without them. PARTLY SUCCEEDS, for the old chain only.

Driftwood checks both out by tag in `propose-tier.yml:164-228`, `shift-left.yml:377-455` and `cut-release.yml:53-121`, and has Flux pins `gotk-sync-nist.yaml` and `gotk-sync-insurer.yaml`, plus Renovate managers for both (`renovate.json:16-24`, `:57-65`, `:99-107`). ## Which attempt succeeded

Attempt 1 succeeded for the loop. Nothing on the target chain reads, imports, pins or schedules either repository. Attempt 2 only finds dependents in the old chain (driftwood pins, composer refusals, ICO's step 8) and the hub truth surface, all of which the proposal parks anyway.

One caveat. This is a surface that ICO feed v5 must keep working without nist. The one thing in ICO that couples to nist is the `source: "nist"` field and the pinned inherits entry. Both are data. Neither needs code.

1. ICO v5 keeps `control_weights` entries as `{id (plain string), weight, basis}` and keeps the editorial basis text ("no regulator publishes an apportionment ... editorial partition", `ico/penalty-schema/v4/feed.json:294`). 2. Decide once whether ICO v5 keeps the `inherits: nist` pin. If it does, the nist remote must stay reachable or `verify-penalty-feed.sh` step 8 must be dropped. Cheapest: keep the pin as data, keep the remote, drop nothing.
3. The rule from insurer commit 2026-10-04: no figure without a priced total. Already in the target (missing number gives a lower bound and `could-not-look`).
4. Nothing else. No number, rung or probe lives in either repository.

## What else would break

- If a person deleted the nist or insurer remotes (cut, not park): driftwood CI on the old chain (`propose-tier`, `shift-left`, `cut-release`, Flux `gotk-sync-*`), ICO `verify-penalty-feed.sh` step 8, hub verify directories listed above, and any Renovate PR for the two pins.
- If composition keeps running at cut-over beside the new loop: it still needs both trees (`composition.py:6574-6577`).
- If the owner counts risk transfer later: the insurer's quote needs a composed exposure total as input (`insurer` commit 2026-10-04). That total comes from the old composer. A returning insurer needs its input rewritten to read the loop's ledger line, not a composed artefact.

## Recommendation: park

Leave both repositories where they are, with workflows and tags, and stop their schedules (cron lines above) once the old chain stops composing. Do not cut: ICO's step 8 and the old chain still resolve their tags. Do not shrink or merge: nothing in them is part of the loop. The one small thing worth carrying is ICO's plain-string control ids, which is already inside the ico repository. Re-admit the insurer only if the owner decides transfer counts as a notch or a lever that moves money. The record does not say that today.

Confidence: high on the code trace (no loop-chain import, pin or read), medium on the hub truth-surface dependents (I did not read every verify script), could not determine whether any repository outside this estate pins the nist or insurer tags.
