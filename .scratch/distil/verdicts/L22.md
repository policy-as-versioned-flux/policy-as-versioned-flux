## Recommendation: **park**

Leave the repositories, tags, default branches and history exactly as they are (park, not cut). The loop runs without them. Nothing in the target reads them. Cutting would remove the only copy of the three items above from the working tree, and the hub's parked truth surface still names them. Parking costs nothing now, and the three fixtures are small enough to copy into platform tests before the schedules are switched off. Not "keep": 92,082 lines of near-clone with no tier ever moved is the drift the owner described. Not "shrink" or "merge": there is no part of either repo the loop needs to run, only fixtures to copy out.
