# L24 Feeds (the whole `feeds` repository)

Read against `fresh/feeds` (remote default branch, 2026-11-14), with consumers read in `fresh/platform` and `fresh/driftwood`. I did not open the target design or the other designs, to stay blind to what the proposal says about this component. All I know of the target is the summary in my task. I read verdict L21 only to copy its layout.

## What is in it (measured)

- 95 tracked files, 85,412 lines (`git ls-files | xargs wc -l`). Two files are 72,785 of those: `fetch/source/cve.json` (58,882, a raw capture) and `cve/v3/feed.json` (13,903).
- Seven feed families: threat-register (v1 to v4), cve (v1 to v3), eol, fx, market-moves, news, recovery-expense. There is also a fetch clock (`fetch/lib.py` 173, `bump.py` 369, six adapters), a release pipeline (`.github/workflows/fetch.yml` 217, `cut-release.yml` 135, `release.yml` 137, `publisher-release-gate.py` 361) and offline checks (`verify-feeds.sh` 420, `verify-market-and-news.sh` 293).
- Tags: threat-register v1.0.0, v2.0.0 (2026-09-01), v3.0.0 (09-10), v4.0.0 (10-03) and v4.1.0 (10-23); cve/v3.0.0 (10-03); fx v1.1.0 and v2.0.0; eol/v2.0.0; recovery-expense v1.0.0, v1.0.1 and v1.0.2 (all 10-23). Read with `git tag -l`.

**The deciding fact.** No published row anywhere in feeds carries a `needs` list. `grep` for `"needs"` or `needs:` across the JSON, YAML and Python found only an English sentence in `cve/v1/feed.json:12`. The threat-row schema is closed: `threat-register/payload.schema.v4.json:169` sets `"additionalProperties": false` on each `threats` row. So adding `needs` is a payload-schema change. Under the estate's own rule (ADR-0019, as quoted in `threat-register/v4/feed.json` changelog: "payload_schema change, so a major") that means a threat-register v5 and a new signed tag. The part of feeds the target keeps is therefore mostly **not yet written**. "Needs lists are in a signed tag dated before any ledger line" is a precondition still to be met, not a fact about today.

## Attempt 1: the loop RUNS WITHOUT it. Partly.

The loop would still run on ico's penalty rows alone, but the figure would be incomplete. About 99% of the repository's lines are not needed. The small part the target keeps is needed.

The target loop's quantify step is this. `loop.py` prices "each published loss row" by what a probe finds open, and `inventory.py` "matches served images to the exploited-flaw feed". In the target, those rows come from two publishers, ico and feeds. Take feeds away and:

- **Threat rows go.** Only feeds publishes per-institution threat rows: an annual frequency and a direct loss per event (`threat-register/v4/feed.json`, `institutions.driftwood.lef [2,4,9]`, `lm_gbp [1000,4000,9000]`, plus the `scheduled-agent-misuses-write-credential` row). ico publishes penalties, not threat events. ico's penalty rows do carry their own frequency, for example `uk-gdpr` `higher-tier` `frequency.lef [0,2,4]`, basis `editorial` (`ico/penalty-schema/v4/feed.json`). So without feeds the loop still has some rows to sum and can produce a figure. That figure would leave out every threat-event row with a direct loss, and every exploited-flaw row.
- **The exploited-flaw feed goes.** The only code in the estate that reads CISA KEV, NVD and FIRST EPSS from the primary sources is `feeds/fetch/cve.py:25-27`. The target's `inventory.py` has nothing to match against. The `cve/v3.0.0` tag is the only signed KEV corpus.
- **The "losses outside the cage" rows** are assigned to feeds in the target summary. Nothing else would hold them.

On today's chain the dependence is concrete and wired:

- `driftwood/party.yaml:72-73` pins `feeds threat-register v4` and `feeds cve v3`; `:68` pins `recovery-expense v1`.
- `driftwood/.github/workflows/propose-tier.yml:203-205` and `shift-left.yml:430-433` check out `policy-as-versioned-feeds/feeds` at the pinned tag.
- `platform/compose/composition.py:831-840` (`_converter`) loads `threat-register/to_fair_scenario.py` from the feeds checkout. `platform/feeds/to_fair_scenario.py:183-215` prices cve against the feeds KEV payload.

What does succeed in Attempt 1: about 99% of the repository's lines are not needed by the target loop.

- The cve raw capture (58,882 lines) and v1/v2 payloads; the v3 payload is needed only as a signed corpus until a fresh fetch replaces it.
- fx (only ludlow, which is parked, signs turnover in USD; driftwood is GBP). Composition reads it implicitly through `_fx_rate` (`composition.py:2002`), but the composer is parked at cut-over.
- eol (pinned only by ludlow).
- market-moves and news (never tagged, pinned by nobody).
- recovery-expense: composition already refuses it in ordinary prices. The predicate is `composition.py:1081-1084` and the refusal is at `:3745-3746`: "recovery-expense is shadow-only; it cannot enter ordinary prices".
- converters/fx.py.
- The reliability adapter ABI, `threat-register/to_fair_scenario.py:295-592` plus `test_widening_cli.py` and `payload.release-cage.schema.v1.json`. The README says no production target exists.
- The native release-proof gate, `publisher-release-gate.py` and its test.
- Threat-register v1 to v3 payloads and schemas.

## Attempt 2: the loop BREAKS or LOSES something without it. Succeeds.

Breaks:

1. **No threat rows, no KEV feed, no outside-the-cage rows.** This is the quantify input for driftwood beyond ico (see Attempt 1).
2. **The adopter's source pin is a parked family's tag.** `driftwood/gitops/flux-system/gotk-sync-feeds.yaml` pins the whole feeds source at `tag: recovery-expense/v1.0.0`, `commit: 9e7958e9...`. `driftwood/renovate.json:85-93` follows the recovery-expense tag family for that pin. The tag stays in history, so nothing breaks the day recovery-expense is parked. But Renovate would never move the pin to a threat-register v5 tag. So the adopter would never pick up the needs lists. The pin and the Renovate rule must move to the family the target reads.
3. **Parking fx, news and market-moves breaks the release gate the target needs to sign v5.**
   - Both `cut-release.yml:94` and `release.yml:97` run `./verify-market-and-news.sh`.
   - `verify-feeds.sh` (run at `cut-release.yml:87` and `release.yml:90`) tests fx (`:261-286`, including `converters/fx.py selfcheck`) and the forward-intel schema. It prints a single pass line that asserts all of them (`:419`).
   - The fetch matrix lists all six feeds (`fetch.yml:68`).
   - So the parked feeds and the gate must be trimmed in the same change. Otherwise the signed tag the question depends on cannot be cut, or must be cut with the parked feeds still in place.
4. **The release gate has a pinned cross-repo dependency.** `verify-feeds.sh:23-34` needs the envelope schema at `platform/feeds/schema.json`. `release.yml:47-48` pins platform `v2.0.1` at `533dccb0...`, and `cut-release.yml:56-61` checks out `ref: v2.0.1`. Both are pinned, so parking platform's working tree will not break them. But a minimal feeds must either carry the envelope schema itself or keep this pin.

Lessons that exist only here (from `git log --format='%ad %h %s' --date=short` and `git show`):

- **07b96d4** (2026-10-23), "Retry CVE response-read timeouts within the existing bounded budget". The real scheduled NVD read timed out inside `response.read()` after the headers arrived. `fetch/cve.py:35-44` retries `TimeoutError` and HTTP 429/502/503 inside one four-attempt budget and never retries a permanent HTTP error. Six offline transport tests are in `.github/tests/test_cve_fetch.py`.
- **`fetch/cve.py:62-63` and `:84-91`**:
  - refuse when NVD returns an empty page before `totalResults`;
  - refuse a KEV entry that has no NVD or no dated EPSS record ("refuses rather than inventing a rate", `:5`);
  - hash every raw response into provenance (`:65`, `:71-73`).
- **5276280** (2026-09-04), "fetch: clean ignored files before the observations switch". `git clean -fd` left the `fetch/__pycache__` that the job itself wrote. The orphan `observations` branch has no `.gitignore`, so the cage step failed every run, and five of six feeds never recorded an observation. The fix is `-ffdx` plus `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE`. Commit message: "a cage refusing its own .pyc looks exactly like a cage refusing a real declaration". The target's ledger branch has the same shape.
- **48b7769** (2026-10-17), "Embed Rekor proof when cutting signed feed releases". `cut-release.yml` needs `-c gitsign.rekorMode=offline`, or the tag cannot later be verified offline.
- **a4919cf** (2026-09-04), "Pin the platform checkout the release gate rules against". Cross-org reads go by a `{tag, commit}` pair, never a default branch (`release.yml:38-48`).
- **ab8af7d** (2026-09-10) and **25214d1** (2026-10-23): reset the declared bump after a cut. Otherwise the next cut computes the wrong major: "the next cut would have computed v4.0.0 for a tree that carries no threat-register/v4".
- **566b93f** (2026-09-01): GitHub did not index the workflows after the default-branch rename until each file was touched.
- **The stall.** `fetch.yml:96-99` exits "already open -- nothing to propose" while a proposal branch exists. Seven `origin/fetch/*` branches are unmerged (`git branch -r --no-merged origin/main`), including `fetch/cve-3.1.0` (last commit 2026-10-05) and `fetch/fx-3.0.0`. So the KEV feed has been frozen at the 2026-10-03 corpus for 42 days. That is a lesson about design, not code: data that must react daily cannot ride a human-merged, hand-cut tag.

Findings against the question someone thought worth checking:

- **"Needs lists and outside-the-cage rows in a signed tag dated before any ledger line":** not met today. No needs list exists, and the schema is closed, so this needs a threat-register v5 tag (major). Adding needs to an existing tag would rewrite history; it must be a new tag.
- **"Rows list fewer needs when unsure":** right direction. Under the target, a row stops counting only when one of its needs is closed. So fewer needs means harder to close, and the figure stays higher. This fits the one rule on numbers. Nothing today encodes it; it needs a sentence in the row schema or README.
- **"No frequency invented by the loop's builders": false on today's data.**
  - driftwood's headline threat row has `lef_basis.kind: editorial` and `lm_basis.kind: editorial`, a "DBIR retail-sector ... editorial midpoint" chosen by the builders (`threat-register/v4/feed.json`, `institutions.driftwood`).
  - Only the agent-misuse row has `lef_basis.kind: published`.
  - On the cve side, the frequency multiplier `annual_events_if_exploited=(1, 2, 6)` times EPSS (`platform/feeds/to_fair_scenario.py:184`) is a builder's invention, and so is `DENY_LEF = (0, 0, 1)` (`:45`). Both live in platform, not feeds.
  - The `severity_lm_gbp` bands in `cve/v3/feed.json:14` are editorial loss bands.
  - So the v5 rows must either drop the editorial frequency or carry it under the one rule on numbers. An unsourced figure may only raise exposure, and the weakest basis is printed. The KEV row must not copy the `(1, 2, 6)` multiplier.
- **"Existing threat-register and cve tags stay":** they can. Parking the working tree removes no tag. The new rows go in v5.
- **"fx, eol, converters, market-moves, news and recovery-expense parked":** safe for driftwood, with two conditions: break 2 (move the source pin off the recovery-expense tag family) and break 3 (trim the release gate and fetch matrix in the same change).

Two more traps carried over from today:

- **Zero KEV matches is not "could not look".** Today driftwood's 418 scanned CVEs have no KEV intersection. `platform/feeds/to_fair_scenario.py:193-196` returns `amount: None`, and the aggregate says NO VERDICT (`driftwood/composed/HANDBOOK.md`, "1 of 4 priced line(s) carry no selected tier ... (cve)"). In the target, a zero match from a fetch and a scan that both worked must count as a measured zero. A failed fetch must give could-not-look.
- **Do not sum overlapping contingencies.** Rows for losses outside the cage must not double-count one incident. This rule is said to live in `driftwood/scenarios/*.json`; I did not re-read those files, so treat it as unverified here.

## Which attempt succeeded

Attempt 2. Without the feeds publisher, the target loop can still run on ico's rows, but it cannot price driftwood's threat-event rows or match served images to exploited flaws. Attempt 1 succeeds only for the roughly 84,000 lines of the repository that the target does not read.

## What the minimal loop must absorb from it

1. A threat-register v5 payload whose rows each carry `needs: [capability...]`. Fewer needs when unsure; empty needs never closes. It must be signed as a new tag before any ledger line reads it.
2. Rows for losses outside the cage, in the same v5 tag, written so that overlapping contingencies are not summed.
3. A basis on every frequency (`published`, `editorial`, ...). Editorial frequencies stay only as figures that raise exposure, never ones that lower it. Drop or relabel the editorial driftwood headline row. No `(1, 2, 6)` multiplier on the KEV row.
4. The KEV fetch core of `fetch/cve.py`: primary URLs, the bounded retry budget for read timeouts and 429/502/503, refuse on an empty page before `totalResults`, refuse on a missing NVD or EPSS record, hash every raw response. The data should be read daily by the loop, or published without waiting on a human merge. Otherwise it freezes again, as `cve/v3.0.0` has since 2026-10-03.
5. Release: gitsign tag with `rekorMode=offline`, identity-pinned verify. Cross-repo reads by `{tag, commit}`. Reset the declared bump after each cut. `-ffdx` clean and `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE` before switching to an orphan ledger or observations branch.
6. Trim `verify-feeds.sh`, `cut-release.yml`, `release.yml` and the `fetch.yml` matrix in the same change that parks fx, eol, market-moves, news and recovery-expense.
7. Budget warning: today's `cut-release.yml` plus `release.yml` are 272 lines, and `fetch/lib.py` plus `bump.py` are 542. The 390-line target for feeds cannot carry them as they stand.

## Recommendation

**SHRINK.** Keep a small, named part:
- threat-register, rewritten as v5 with needs lists and outside-the-cage rows;
- the KEV fetch core of `fetch/cve.py`;
- a trimmed cut-release with offline Rekor;
- one envelope check.

Park the rest in history at cut-over: cve raw capture and v1/v2, fx, eol, converters, market-moves, news, recovery-expense, the adapter ABI, the native release-proof gate, threat-register v1 to v3 working copies, and `verify-market-and-news.sh`. All existing tags stay.

Not keep: about 99% of the lines are not on the target chain. Not park or cut: the target's quantify step reads this repository. Not merge: the target names feeds as its own publisher, and folding it into ico would mix two publishers' signing identities.

Confidence high that the loop needs a small part of feeds and not the rest. Medium on whether 390 lines is enough, given break 3 and the 272-line release pair.
