# P1 - platform/computed-semver, platform/distribution, platform/compose, root verify-*/lib* scripts

Read-only review against FRESH (clones of 2026-11-14). All paths are under `FRESH/platform` unless prefixed. Measured with `wc -l`. Judged only by what each part does for the loop Q-T-C-R-E-X.

## Summary

- Area: 436 files, 43,172 lines (computed-semver 313 files / 14,507; distribution 80 / 6,998; compose 35 / 19,568; root verify-*.sh, verify-release-signing.py and lib*.sh 8 / 2,099). `verify-release-signing.py` (89 lines) is not a `.sh`; I counted it because it is a root `verify-*` script.
- **computed-semver computes** a major/minor/patch label for a policy release. It evaluates every supported prior policy version and the candidate with the real `kyverno` CLI against about 180 generated pods, observes how the "cage spec" each pod receives moves (`computed-semver/cage_engine.py:1`), and signs an evidence JSON. The loop's E step does not read it. Its job is to refuse a release whose declared number is weaker than the computed one.
- **distribution distributes** one Flux `ResourceSet` (`distribution/versions.yaml:153` onward) that fans out, per declared policy version, a tag-pinned `GitRepository` and a pruning `Kustomization` over `distribution/policies/vN`. Those trees are the Kyverno cage bodies (cage-tier, cage-netpol, PriorityClasses, require-nonroot, stamp-posture). The same template adds eight inline "platform-machinery" documents (orphan guard and cage, governed-namespace guard, bottom-rung netpol). This is the nearest thing in the area to E.
- **compose composes** an adopter's `party.yaml` parents (platform policy versions, NIST controls, ICO penalty schema, threat/CVE/EOL/FX feeds, an insurer quote) into a signed `composed/` tree plus an `evidence.json` that prices every parent in money and proposes a cage tier per line. It is the only part of the area that does Q, T, C and the R proposal (`compose/composition.py:3719` `price_parent`, `:5179` `aggregate_section`, calling `graded/cage.py` `select_tier` `:334`). About 59% of `composition.py` (lines 1-1546 and 5662-10142) is parent-loading, docs, orchestration and self-test, not pricing.
- computed-semver by lines: 5,411 (37%) is fixtures, generated corpus and signed evidence; 8,496 (59%) is Python, of which 498 are test files and at least 2,405 are `selfcheck` functions embedded in the modules, leaving about 5,600 production lines; 564 are shell wrappers and a README; 36 are config.
- Verdict: the loop's Q-T-C-R wiring in this area is about 2,500 lines of `composition.py` (plus `graded/cage.py`, outside the area). E needs roughly 200 lines of this area (one served policy tree's three cage bodies plus one Flux pin). The remaining ~40,000 lines are release-number honesty, multi-version coexistence, insurer/quote/handbook/retirement detail, and self-tests.

## What the loop would lose if this area vanished

- **Q, T, C and the R proposal would lose their only wiring.** The per-feed price code, the read of the party's signed appetite, the sum against the band and the proposed tier (`composition.py` 1898-2562, 3403-4750 and 5179-5661, about 2,500 lines) call `graded/cage.py` (outside this area, 668 lines) and the feed converters. Without compose, nothing turns a feed into a per-line GBP residual compared with `appetite.tolerance`. Evidence it ran: `driftwood/composed/evidence.json:379` (tolerance 40000) and `:5752` (a line whose "caged residual (530.83 GBP) is within the tolerance" is clamped up to the declared floor `isolated`).
- **E would lose its cage bodies and their Flux pin.** `distribution/policies/v7.0.0` and the per-version `GitRepository`/`Kustomization` template are the only things in the area that put a cage on a running cluster. The tier itself is set elsewhere: `platform/wargamer/tier_pr.py:1` opens a PR editing `posture.acme.io/tier` on the adopter's governed Namespace manifest, and the served `cage-tier` body reads that label through `namespaceObject` (`distribution/policies/v5.0.0/cage-tier.yaml`, variable `nsTier`). So distribution delivers the ladder; the notch is set outside this area.
- **The loop would lose nothing from computed-semver.** The bump computation, generated corpus, coverage counts and standing check are not read by Q, T, C, R, E or X. What would disappear is a refusal when a release's version number understates how much it changed the cage. Real releases do not exercise it: the records say `major` for 4.0.0 to 7.0.0 and `none` for 2.0.1 and 3.0.0 (`computed-semver/evidence/*.json`, field `bump`). No release has computed minor or patch.
- X (react) has no component here. Nothing in this area watches the world; feeds enter via `compose/composition.py:860` `vendor_feed` only when a pin moves.

## The simplest thing that would do this area's loop job

One module, `loop_step.py`, about 250 lines, replacing the area's decision and delivery path:

1. `price(feed_payload, tolerance, floor) -> {exposure_gbp, residual_by_tier, tier}`: one function over `graded/cage.py`'s existing `TIERS` table (`:82`) and `select_tier` (`:334`). Replaces `composition.py` 1898-2562, 3403-4304 and 5179-5661 (about 2,050 lines) with about 120.
2. `breach(prices, tolerance) -> bool`: sum the selected-tier residuals, return true if over tolerance. This is `aggregate_section` (`composition.py:5179`) without its docstring, about 40 lines.
3. One Namespace label write (what `tier_pr.py` does; 1,794 lines, outside the area) and one Flux `GitRepository`+`Kustomization` pointing at a signed tag containing the three cage bodies (about 170 lines of YAML, taken from `policies/v7.0.0`).
4. For "does the new release tighten the cage": one test comparing dial values tier by tier, each at least as tight as the previous release (about 60 lines), instead of the 14,507-line corpus-and-engine gate.
5. Keep as-is: the signing identity checks (`verify-certificate-identity-regexp.sh`, 45 lines) and the `lib.sh` could-not-look convention (102 lines).

Rough total: 250 lines of logic plus about 170 lines of YAML, against 43,172 lines in the area today. The count excludes the feed converters and `graded/cage.py`, which another area covers.

## Surprises

- **The elaborate gate has only ever answered "major".** Records: 4.0.0 to 7.0.0 declared major, computed major (`computed-semver/evidence/{4,5,6,7}.0.0.json`); 2.0.1 declared patch, computed none; 3.0.0 declared major, computed none. After the corpus, witnesses, coverage and standing check (about 5,600 production lines plus 5,411 data lines), the discrimination actually used is "any change to the dial table is a major". `hub/.scratch/computed-semver/map.md` records the blind spot itself: the cage "ratchets with no counter-pressure" and "removing enforcement scores as a patch".
- **The standing check disagrees with a recorded release.** `hub/talk/captures/_grades.tsv:42` grades PASS on a note that 7.0.0's recorded `major` "now recomputes as 'patch' under generator 0.3.0", the same generator version. The check is designed to print, not fail. A possible cause: `generator_standing_check.py:163-188` re-runs against the single immediate predecessor while the publisher gate compared against the whole window (`evidence/7.0.0.json` old=174, new=140). I did not confirm this and did not run it. Could not determine.
- **Every served release is an 80% copy.** v5.0.0, v6.0.0, v7.0.0 are 319, 319 and 279 lines; between neighbours each file changes 6 to 18 lines, mostly version labels. Four more retired trees (1,014 lines) stay on disk. The owner's "forward and back by one" coexistence premise costs about 1,900 lines of near-duplicate YAML plus the 1,683-line renderer set.
- **The ratchet is pinned at the bottom rung.** In the driftwood evidence the loosest tier that fits the tolerance is chosen and then "clamped up to the party's declared floor 'isolated'" (`driftwood/composed/evidence.json:5752`, `:5878`). The declared floor, not the risk comparison, decides the tier there; there is no further notch to turn.
- **Two delivery routes to one cluster.** `driftwood/gitops/platform/platform-distribution.yaml` (platform's ResourceSet) and `driftwood/gitops/composed/composed-set.yaml` (compose's output) both fan out per-version Kustomizations, and the composed set keeps its own copy of the version array.
- **One 10,142-line file, 35% self-test.** `composition.py` holds 3,570 lines after line 6573, of which `selfcheck()` alone is 3,011. The docstring and constants (601 lines) are mostly ticket narrative. With the README (717) and two contract docs, prose is 1,507 lines; the pricing proper is about 2,500.
- **Quantify has side-channels.** About 4,500 lines of compose (quote assessment 1,760, publisher reliability 847, twin-agent misuse 446, handbook 1,493) refine or present the money number rather than produce it. The quote and reliability modules were added 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-23. Only platform PRs 73 and 74 (2026-10-25) carry a `codex/` branch name; I did not attribute the rest.
- **The computed-semver premise has a dated owner reframe but no owner ask for the gate.** The owner's 2026-08-20 words ("you're always caged even if it's a permissive one. It's the spec of the cage that can change", `hub/.scratch/computed-semver/issues/02-what-counts-as-a-verdict.md:54`) shaped what is compared. The gate itself comes from the map the assistant charted the same day (`hub/.scratch/computed-semver/map.md`, "Destination") on the PRD's semver thesis, so origin is `assistant`, not `owner_words`.
- **Live evidence is thin.** In truth run 491 (2026-11-12) the offline proofs pass, while `verify-coexistence.sh`, `verify-declared-versions-admit.sh`, `verify-retirement.sh` and `verify-source-verification.sh` read SKIP (no kind cluster). The README's live observations on `kind-driftwood` (2026-08-28/29) concern retired lines; I could not corroborate them from a recorded run.
- **Birth dates are split dates.** `git log` for `distribution/versions.yaml`, `render-orphan-guard.py`, `policies/v1.0.0` and `README.md` all read 2026-07-31, the first commit of this repo. Earlier authorship: could not determine.
