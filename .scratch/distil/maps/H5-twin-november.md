## Plain words: what problem each new module solves, for whom, and whether pricing or the cage calls it

The shared story. The twin has six "skills" (signal-classify, causal-claims, evolution-judge, substrate-generator, gameplay-lens, ethics-gate). Today all six are keyword heuristics: `twin/skill-scores.jsonl` has 20 rows, all `heuristic-0.1.0` (first row `"model_version": "heuristic-0.1.0"`, score 1.0 on its own corpus). ADR-0029 (2026-09-22) says a language model may only take over a skill after a measured "permission": a labelled corpus, a score strictly above the best constant answer, pinned weights. The calibration programme builds the apparatus to measure that for Qwen3-4B on a CPU, offline. The "for whom" of every module below is therefore one reader: the owner or operator who wants the twin to run judgement steps unattended on a clock. Not one module serves an adopter, an insurer, an auditor of the cage, or the money number.
