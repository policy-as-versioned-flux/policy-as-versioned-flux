## What the loop would lose if this area vanished

- It would lose almost nothing it runs today. The tiers that are priced and tightened live in platform and the adopters; none of the build-guard, people-sensing, verdict or signing components is imported by them.
- It would lose, inside the twin: the evidence-grade price gate (`evidence.may_price`), the constraint pre-filter data, causal admission, and the corroboration read by `pricing.py:368`. Those are real quantify-side guards, about 2,100 lines, and they are what makes a pound figure defensible.
- It would lose the refusal that stops a model clock from judging until it is measured (`model_permission`), but today that refusal protects nothing from running (0 of 14 permitted).
- It would lose the assistant's leash (enact_guard) and merge identity (other_hand). The leash is switched to development by the owner's standing words, so loss is of an option, not of present behaviour; the merge identity is observed in use (218 bot commits) and its loss would change how PRs are merged, not what the loop computes.
- It would lose no T and no C, because they are not here.

## The simplest thing that would do this area's loop job

The loop job (keep a priced figure honest; hold a response back until enactment is evidenced; stop an unmeasured model driving the react step) is three small things in the pricing path:

1. One function `may_price(grade, threshold)` and a five-row grade table, about 40 lines (what `evidence.py:148` and `evidence-ladder.yaml` already do, minus the git-history regrade check, 300 lines).
2. One list of forbidden option ids checked before pricing, about 30 lines (what `constraints.py` and `options.py:11` do, without the scope-exclusion and position machinery).
3. One function `enacted(response_id) -> bool` returning true only when two independent channels agree, about 40 lines, replacing 607 lines of corroboration and a six-channel table with `alone:` grades. The model permission becomes one boolean per skill read from the score log, about 40 lines.

Everything else is either the build's leash (replace with the server-side ruleset the docstring already names as the upgrade, `enact_guard.py:34` "ponytail" paragraph, and a one-line owner statement of the mode) or a capability the loop does not have. Target: 150 to 200 lines in place of 2,722 for the loop-guarding set; the other 7,600 lines either go to a different repo or are deleted.
